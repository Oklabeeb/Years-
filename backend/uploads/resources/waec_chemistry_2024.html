Question 1

What volume of 0.5 mol/dm³ HCl is required to neutralize 25.0 cm³ of 0.2 mol/dm³ Na₂CO₃ solution?

  1. 10.0 cm³
  2. 20.0 cm³
  3. 25.0 cm³
  4. 50.0 cm³
Solution: Equation: Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂. Moles of base = (C_b × V_b)/1000 = (0.2 × 25)/1000 = 0.005 mol. Mole ratio base:acid = 1:2 ⇒ Moles of acid = 0.010 mol. V_acid = Moles / C_a = (0.010 / 0.5) × 1000 = 20.0 cm³.
Correct Answer: B

Themes from the Old Testament, the Synoptic Gospels and the Apostolic Church

Question 1

According to the creation account in Genesis 1, on which day were the sun, moon, and stars created?

  1. First day
  2. Second day
  3. Third day
  4. Fourth day
Explanation: Genesis 1:14-19 records that the heavenly luminaries (sun, moon, and stars) were set in the firmament of heaven on the Fourth Day.
Correct Answer: D

Question 2

Discuss the leadership qualities of Joseph during the seven years of famine in Egypt and highlight two lessons for modern civil administrators.

Solution: Joseph exhibited proactive foresight, meticulous grain rationing, anti-corruption inventory control, and equitable resource allocation. Lessons: (1) Strategic national planning during periods of economic surplus; (2) Accountable stewardship in public treasury management.

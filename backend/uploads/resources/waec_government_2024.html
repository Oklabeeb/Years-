Question 1

Which political doctrine advocates that powers should be divided among the legislature, executive, and judiciary to avoid tyranny?

  1. Rule of Law
  2. Separation of Powers
  3. Collective Responsibility
  4. Sovereignty
Explanation: Formulated by Baron de Montesquieu in "The Spirit of the Laws" (1748).
Correct Answer: B

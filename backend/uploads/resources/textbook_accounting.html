Chapter 1: The Golden Rules of Double-Entry

For every debit entry, there must be a corresponding credit entry. Rule: Debit the receiver, Credit the giver. Debit what comes in, Credit what goes out. Debit all expenses and losses, Credit all incomes and gains.

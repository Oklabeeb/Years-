Section 1: Lexis and Structure

Choose the word that is nearest in meaning to the underlined word:
The manager's decision was irrevocable once ratified by the board.

  1. unwise
  2. unalterable
  3. unilateral
  4. temporary
Explanation: "Irrevocable" denotes a verdict or act that cannot be revoked, recalled, or changed. Synonym: unalterable.
Correct Answer: B

Section 2: Sentence Completion

Neither the headmaster nor the subject teachers ______ present at the assembly.

  1. was
  2. were
  3. is
  4. has been
Explanation: By proximity concord with correlative conjunction "neither...nor", the verb agrees with the closer subject ("the subject teachers" - plural). Hence, "were" is correct.
Correct Answer: B

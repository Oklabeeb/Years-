Chapter 1: Measurement & Physical Quantities

Physics is the study of matter in relation to energy. Quantities are classified as fundamental (Length, Mass, Time, Current, Temperature) and derived (Velocity, Force, Pressure, Energy).

Dimensional Analysis: Every physical equation must be dimensionally homogeneous. [Velocity] = LT⁻¹, [Acceleration] = LT⁻², [Force] = MLT⁻², [Work/Energy] = ML²T⁻².

Chapter 2: Linear Motion & Kinematics

Equations of uniformly accelerated linear motion: (1) v = u + at, (2) s = ut + ½at², (3) v² = u² + 2as.

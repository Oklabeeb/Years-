Question 1

A projectile is launched from ground level with a velocity of 50 m/s at an angle of 30° to the horizontal. Calculate the maximum height reached. (Take g = 10 m/s²).

  1. 31.25 m
  2. 62.50 m
  3. 125.00 m
  4. 250.00 m
Solution: H_max = (u² sin²θ) / (2g) = (50² × sin²30°) / (2 × 10) = (2500 × 0.25) / 20 = 625 / 20 = 31.25 m.
Correct Answer: A

Question 2

State Newton's Second Law of Motion and show that F = ma.

Solution: The rate of change of momentum of an object is directly proportional to the applied resultant force and occurs in the direction of the force. F ∝ (mv - mu)/t = m(v - u)/t = ma. With constant of proportionality k = 1 in SI units, F = ma.

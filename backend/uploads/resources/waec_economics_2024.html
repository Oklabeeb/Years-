Question 1

When the price of a commodity falls from ₦200 to ₦160, the quantity demanded rises from 40 units to 60 units. Calculate the price elasticity of demand.

  1. 1.5
  2. 2.0
  3. 2.5
  4. 0.5
Solution: % Change in Q = (20 / 40) × 100 = 50%. % Change in P = (40 / 200) × 100 = 20%. Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) = %ΔQ / %ΔP = 50 / 20 = 2.5 (Elastic).
Correct Answer: C

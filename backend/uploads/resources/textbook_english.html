Chapter 1: Grammatical Concord & Subject-Verb Agreement

Concord refers to the grammatical agreement between parts of a sentence. Essential rules include:

  • Rule of Number: A singular subject takes a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb. e.g., The boy plays; the boys play.
  • Proximity Concord: With correlatives (either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also), the verb agrees with the nearest subject.
  • Parenthetical Concord: Expressions like "as well as", "together with", "in addition to" do not alter the number of the initial subject. e.g., The principal, together with his staff, is attending the summit.

Chapter 2: Formal Essay & Argumentative Writing

An argumentative essay requires clear thesis statement, logical evidence, refutation of counter-arguments, and persuasive conclusion. Maintain formal register throughout.

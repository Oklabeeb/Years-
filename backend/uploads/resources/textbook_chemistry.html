Chapter 1: Atomic Structure and Periodic Table

Atoms consist of protons and neutrons in the nucleus with electrons orbiting in energy levels. Electronic configurations follow the Aufbau principle, Hund's rule, and Pauli exclusion principle.

Chapter 2: Chemical Bonding & Stoichiometry

Electrovalent (ionic) bonding involves electron transfer between metals and non-metals; covalent bonding involves electron sharing between non-metals.

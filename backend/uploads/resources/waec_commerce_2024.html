Question 1

Which of the following aids to trade is primarily concerned with creating time utility for produced goods?

  1. Advertising
  2. Banking
  3. Warehousing
  4. Insurance
Explanation: Warehousing stores goods until they are demanded, bridging the time gap between production and consumption.
Correct Answer: C

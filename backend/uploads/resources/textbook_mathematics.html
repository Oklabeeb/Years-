Chapter 1: Indices and Logarithms

The rules of indices form the bedrock of algebraic manipulation:

  • Multiplication Rule: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
  • Division Rule: aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ
  • Power Rule: (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
  • Zero Index: a⁰ = 1 (where a ≠ 0)
  • Negative Index: a⁻ᵐ = 1/aᵐ
  • Fractional Index: a^(m/n) = ⁿ√(aᵐ)
Key Law of Logarithms: log_b(xy) = log_b(x) + log_b(y); log_b(x/y) = log_b(x) - log_b(y); log_b(xⁿ) = n·log_b(x).

Chapter 2: Quadratic Equations and Functions

Any equation in the form ax² + bx + c = 0 (where a ≠ 0) is a quadratic equation. It can be solved by factorization, completing the square, or using the quadratic formula: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / (2a).

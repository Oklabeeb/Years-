Question 1

An electric heater rated 2 kW, 250 V is operated for 5 hours daily. Calculate the cost of operating the heater for 30 days if electrical energy costs ₦50 per kWh.

  1. ₦15,000
  2. ₦12,500
  3. ₦18,000
  4. ₦9,000
Solution: Total Energy = Power × Time = 2 kW × (5 h × 30 days) = 2 × 150 = 300 kWh. Total Cost = 300 kWh × ₦50 = ₦15,000.
Correct Answer: A

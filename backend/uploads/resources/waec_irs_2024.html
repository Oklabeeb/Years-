Question 1

Suratul Fatihah is described in the Holy Quran as "Sab'an min al-Mathani" which translates to:

  1. The seven oft-repeated verses
  2. The seven golden pillars
  3. The seven virtues
  4. The seven miraculous signs
Explanation: In Surah Al-Hijr (15:87), Allah refers to Suratul Fatihah as the seven oft-repeated verses (Sab'an min al-Mathani).
Correct Answer: A

Question 2

Examine the socio-economic benefits of Zakat in mitigating poverty within an Islamic community.

Solution: Zakat serves as a mandatory institutional wealth-redistribution mechanism, preventing capital hoarding (kanz), fostering compassion between wealthy and vulnerable citizens, providing debt relief (Al-Gharimin), and stimulating local commerce.

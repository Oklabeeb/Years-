Question 1

Which fundamental human right guarantees that every Nigerian citizen has freedom to express opinions and hold beliefs without interference?

  1. Right to Life
  2. Right to Freedom of Expression and the Press
  3. Right to Fair Hearing
  4. Right to Private Family Life
Explanation: Protected under Section 39 of the 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
Correct Answer: B

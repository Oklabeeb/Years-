Question 1

Which of the following cell organelles is responsible for aerobic cellular respiration and ATP synthesis?

  1. Ribosome
  2. Mitochondrion
  3. Chloroplast
  4. Golgi Apparatus
Explanation: The mitochondrion is the powerhouse of the cell where the Krebs cycle and oxidative phosphorylation yield ATP.
Correct Answer: B

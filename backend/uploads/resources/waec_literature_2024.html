Question 1

In literary appreciation, what figure of speech is demonstrated in: "The wind whispered softly through the ancient trees"?

  1. Hyperbole
  2. Personification
  3. Metaphor
  4. Oxymoron
Explanation: Personification attributes human qualities (whispering) to inanimate objects (the wind).
Correct Answer: B

Question 1

According to the basic accounting equation, if Total Assets = ₦1,500,000 and Total Liabilities = ₦600,000, what is the Owner's Equity?

  1. ₦2,100,000
  2. ₦900,000
  3. ₦600,000
  4. ₦1,500,000
Solution: Assets = Liabilities + Capital (Owner's Equity) ⇒ Capital = Assets - Liabilities = ₦1,500,000 - ₦600,000 = ₦900,000.
Correct Answer: B

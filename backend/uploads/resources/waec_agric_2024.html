Question 1

Which soil nutrient is primarily responsible for vigorous vegetative leaf growth in cereal crops?

  1. Phosphorus
  2. Nitrogen
  3. Potassium
  4. Magnesium
Explanation: Nitrogen stimulates chlorophyll synthesis and vegetative growth; phosphorus aids root and seed development; potassium governs disease resistance.
Correct Answer: B

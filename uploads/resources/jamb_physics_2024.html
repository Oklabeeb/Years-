Question 1

A body starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at \( 4\,\text{m/s}^2 \) for 6 seconds. What is the distance covered in the third second?

  1. 10 m
  2. 14 m
  3. 18 m
  4. 24 m
Solution: Distance in the \( n^{\text{th}} \) second is given by \( s_n = u + \frac{1}{2}a(2n - 1) \).
Here \( u = 0, a = 4, n = 3 \).
\( s_3 = 0 + \frac{1}{2}(4)(2 \times 3 - 1) = 2(5) = 10\,\text{m} \).
Correct Answer: A
Question 2

The half-life of a radioactive isotope is 12 days. How much of a 32 g sample will remain after 48 days?

  1. 8 g
  2. 4 g
  3. 2 g
  4. 1 g
Solution: Number of half-lives \( n = \frac{48}{12} = 4 \).
Remaining mass = \( 32 \times (\frac{1}{2})^4 = 32 \times \frac{1}{16} = 2\,\text{g} \).
Correct Answer: C

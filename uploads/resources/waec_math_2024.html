SECTION A: OBJECTIVE QUESTIONS

Question 1

Evaluate \( \log_{10} 25 + \log_{10} 4 - \log_{10} 0.1 \).

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4
Solution: \( \log_{10}(25 \times 4) - \log_{10}(10^{-1}) = \log_{10}(100) - (-1) = 2 + 1 = 3 \).
Correct Answer: C
Question 2

If \( 3^{2x+1} - 28(3^x) + 9 = 0 \), solve for \( x \).

  1. \( x = -1 \) or \( x = 2 \)
  2. \( x = 1 \) or \( x = -2 \)
  3. \( x = 0 \) or \( x = 3 \)
  4. \( x = 2 \) or \( x = 4 \)
Solution: Let \( y = 3^x \). Then \( 3y^2 - 28y + 9 = 0 \implies (3y - 1)(y - 9) = 0 \implies y = \frac{1}{3} \) or \( y = 9 \). Therefore \( 3^x = 3^{-1} \implies x = -1 \), and \( 3^x = 3^2 \implies x = 2 \).
Correct Answer: A
Question 3

A bag contains 5 red balls and 7 green balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that both balls are of the same colour.

  1. \( \frac{31}{66} \)
  2. \( \frac{10}{33} \)
  3. \( \frac{7}{22} \)
  4. \( \frac{15}{66} \)
Solution: \( P(\text{Both Red}) = \frac{5}{12} \times \frac{4}{11} = \frac{20}{132} \).
\( P(\text{Both Green}) = \frac{7}{12} \times \frac{6}{11} = \frac{42}{132} \).
Total = \( \frac{20 + 42}{132} = \frac{62}{132} = \frac{31}{66} \).
Correct Answer: A

SECTION B: THEORY & APPLICATION

Question 4 (12 Marks)

The third term of an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is 10 and the eighth term is 25.

(a) Find the first term \( a \) and the common difference \( d \).
(b) Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the progression.

Solution:
(a) \( T_3 = a + 2d = 10 \), \( T_8 = a + 7d = 25 \).
Subtracting: \( 5d = 15 \implies d = 3 \).
Substitute \( d \): \( a + 2(3) = 10 \implies a = 4 \).
(b) \( S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}[2(4) + (20 - 1)(3)] = 10[8 + 57] = 10(65) = 650 \).
